Makes up names for science fiction, each with a button that adds it to the encyclopedia. Run it from Project Home or from an encyclopedia entry.

It has three actions. Sci-fi characters makes alien names (harsh, flowing, hissing, or clipped), handles, and machine designations, added as people. Sci-fi worlds makes names that sound like the stars and moons, or of colonies and stations, or catalogue designations, added as places. Sci-fi things names ships and companies, added as things. Each makes up to 20 at a time, and characters and things can come in a long form.

The star names are those the International Astronomical Union has approved, shared under CC BY 4.0, and the moons' are from Wikidata; the package's `resources/NOTICE.txt` says more.
