Reports the level each scene and the whole story reads at, from early reader to dense, as US school grades worked out from sentence and word length. Run it from Project Home for the whole story or chosen scenes, or from a scene.

The report lists each scene's rare words and the scenes that read well above or below the rest. It works with English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, and Ukrainian projects, each by its own formula and list of common words, and leaves encyclopedia names out of word length.

The common word lists are built from FrequencyWords, from OpenSubtitles 2018, and shared under CC BY-SA 4.0; the package's `resources/NOTICE.txt` says more.
