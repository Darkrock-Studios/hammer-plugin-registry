Exports the story as the HTML [Archive of Our Own](https://archiveofourown.org)'s work text box takes, a chapter at a time, ready to paste.

Each chapter comes under a marker line such as `===== Chapter 2 of 12: The Pool of Tears =====`. Paste what is under it into the chapter's text, in AO3's HTML editor, and the name into its chapter title. Italics, bold, links, lists and the rest are written as the tags AO3 allows, and the text is escaped so nothing in the prose is read as markup.

Its setting chooses the scene break: a horizontal line, a centered `* * *`, or a blank line.
