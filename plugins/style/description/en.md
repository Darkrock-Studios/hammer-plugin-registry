Reports readability, adverbs, filter words, dialogue, and repeated words and phrases, for the whole story and each scene, as a document you can keep as a note. Run it from Project Home for the whole story or the scenes you choose.

It counts English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, and Ukrainian projects by their own rules and word lists, and writes the report in the language of Hammer's interface. Its settings choose whether filter words are counted, whether each scene gets its own section, how often a word repeats before it is listed, and how long each list is.

Some word lists are built from a subtitle frequency list and spell-check dictionaries, and shared under their terms; the package's `resources/NOTICE.txt` lists them.
