Grammar, punctuation, and style checks from [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org), underlined in the editor as you write, in more than 30 languages.

The text you write is sent over the internet to be checked. In its settings you choose where: your Hammer sync server if it has grammar checking enabled, LanguageTool's free service, LanguageTool Premium with your account's email and API key, or a LanguageTool server of your own.

Its settings also control picky checks of style and typography, and style suggestions for wordiness, redundancy, and register, which are underlined more quietly than mistakes.
