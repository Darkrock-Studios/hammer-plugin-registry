Grammar, punctuation, and style checks from [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org), underlined in the editor as you write, in more than 30 languages.

The text you write is sent over the internet to be checked. In its settings you choose where: LanguageTool's free service, LanguageTool Premium with your account's email and API key, or a LanguageTool server of your own. Text is sent in batches within the service's limits, and each paragraph's results are kept by its text, so a scene you open again is not sent again.

The settings also turn on picky checks of style and typography, and style suggestions for wordiness, redundancy, and register, which are underlined more quietly than mistakes.
