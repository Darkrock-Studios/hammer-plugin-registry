Interviews a character, a page of questions at a time, then writes the answers into a new encyclopedia entry for them and opens it. Run it from Project Home and give the character's name.

There are three sets of questions: a quick one of 12, a full one of 33, and the 31 questions of the confession album Marcel Proust filled in around 1890, in an English wording of the plugin's own. If you close the dialog part way through, the interview is kept, and running it again picks up where you left off.
