Keeps the names in your story in line with the encyclopedia. As you write, it underlines words spelled close to an entry's name, and names written in lower case.

The Names report, from Project Home, lists where each entry is mentioned, likely misspellings, and names used often that have no entry. Only names the project language's word list does not know are checked, so a name that is also a word, such as "Grace", is left to spell check. It ships word lists for English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, and Ukrainian, and checks only languages it has a list for.

The word lists other than English are built from a subtitle frequency list and spell-check dictionaries, and shared under their terms; the package's `resources/words/NOTICE.txt` lists them.
