Suggests real names from a culture and a decade of birth, picked as often as they were given, each with a button that adds it to the encyclopedia as a person. Run it from Project Home or from an encyclopedia entry.

There are 26 cultures, and decades of birth from the 1880s to the 2020s. You choose feminine, masculine, or any given names, common, rare, or a realistic mix, how many to make (up to 20), and whether they come with surnames. The name lists, with how many people had each name, are built from public data.

The package's `resources/names/NOTICE.txt` lists each source and its terms. The Chinese lists are for non-commercial use only.
