English grammar and style checks from [Harper](https://writewithharper.com/), underlined in the editor as you write. Harper runs entirely locally, so your text is not sent anywhere, and works offline.

Settings available to control specific rules: grammar (agreement, word order, "a" or "an", and repeated words), word choice, punctuation and capitalization, style (wordiness, redundancy, and readability), and sentences over 40 words.

Harper is thorough but large: it needs about 100 MB of memory.
