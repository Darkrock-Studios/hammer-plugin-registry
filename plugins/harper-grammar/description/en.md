English grammar and style checks from [Harper](https://writewithharper.com/), underlined in the editor as you write. Harper runs inside the plugin, so your text is not sent anywhere.

Its settings turn each group of checks on or off: grammar (agreement, word order, "a" or "an", and repeated words), word choice, punctuation and capitalization, style (wordiness, redundancy, and readability), and sentences over 40 words.

Harper is thorough but large: it needs about 100 MB of memory, and the plugin asks Hammer for a limit of 256 MB.
